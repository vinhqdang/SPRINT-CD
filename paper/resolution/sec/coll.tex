\section{No rule leads across collections}\label{sec:coll}

\paragraph{(1) The leader changes with the collection.} The best rule is faithful compression on T\"ubingen ($66.7\%$; LOCI and TRA at $64.7$ and $64.6\%$), RECI on both chamber collections with independent or random-walk actuators ($97.9\%$ and $94.3\%$), our first compression implementation on the 17 Sachs pairs ($76.5\%$; the faithful one $64.7\%$), pairwise LiNGAM on the Bernoulli collection ($87.5\%$), RECI and the faithful compression together on the changepoint collection ($100\%$ for both), and IGCI on the Malus collection ($95.8\%$). No rule is above $70\%$ on more than three collections, and five of the twelve rules lead at least one collection.

\paragraph{(2) Rules that agree on one collection can disagree on the next.} IGCI is the second-best rule on both chamber collections ($93.6$ and $88.5\%$) and the best on Malus, yet it orients every one of the $16$ Bernoulli pairs and every one of the $70$ changepoint pairs the wrong way ($0\%$): the cause has many ties (a grid of $101$ levels with about $20$ rows per level in the Bernoulli collection, four piecewise-constant levels in each changepoint experiment), and we conjecture, without having diagnosed it, that the entropy estimator is then degenerate after the small jitter that breaks the ties, so that the decision is the same for every pair. RECI, best or tied-best on three collections, is at $25\%$ on the Bernoulli pairs. AVICI is at $90.4\%$ on the i.i.d.\ chambers and at $2.1\%$ on Malus, where it prefers the reverse direction for $47$ of the $48$ pairs. Compression is at $100\%$ on the changepoint pairs and at $38.3\%$ on the i.i.d.\ chambers.

\paragraph{(3) Rankings of rules do not transfer.} The Kendall correlation between the ordering of the rules on T\"ubingen and on the i.i.d.\ chambers is $-0.38$, and $-0.30$ against the walk pairs; the two chamber collections agree with each other ($+0.53$); T\"ubingen is uncorrelated with Sachs ($-0.12$). A rule that leads the benchmark most often used for pairs says little about its behaviour on a collection from a device whose graph is documented. With twelve rules and intervals of $\pm 0.10$ individual correlations are themselves uncertain, but their sign pattern is not what a shared ranking would produce.

\paragraph{Cautions.} The Bernoulli, changepoint and Malus collections consist of $8$, $7$ and $4$ distinct edges, repeated across seeds or experiments, with the same cause variable (\texttt{load\_in}, or the two polarisers); they are small, correlated sets of regimes, not samples of mechanisms, and we use them as such. Sachs has $17$ pairs. The T\"ubingen collection mixes pairs from several fields and its weights downweight groups of similar pairs; unweighted accuracy is used here unless stated.
